### Experimental Design

The study examined how human intestinal tissue responds to *Salmonella typhi* infection. The experiment used human terminal ileum biopsies, where the manipulated condition was exposure to *S. typhi* strain Ty2, while non-infected biopsies served as the control. The infected biopsies were exposed to the bacteria for 2 hours before RNA sequencing was performed. RNA-seq was used to measure changes in gene expression during the early stage of infection. The experiment included biological replicates for the infected and non-infected tissue conditions, allowing the researchers to compare gene expression between the treatment and control samples.
